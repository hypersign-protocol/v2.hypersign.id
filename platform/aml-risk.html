Detection Layers

Multiple Detection Layers. One Clear Verdict.

AML risk does not come from a single direction. Hypersign applies independent screening layers so that a subject who does not appear on one source is still caught by another.

Layer 1 Sanctions

OFAC SDN, OFAC Consolidated, EU Consolidated, UN Security Council, HMT Financial Sanctions, G20 national lists, and custom lists checked simultaneously.

Layer 2 Political Exposure

Four-level PEP classification with relatives and close associates. Source references returned for every match to support documented compliance decisions.

Layer 3 Adverse Media

Global and regional news, court records, regulatory announcements, and enforcement publications scanned across six risk categories.

Layer 4 Enforcement & Regulatory

Regulatory enforcement orders, licence revocations, fines, censures, and fitness-and-probity flags published by financial regulators across 200+ jurisdictions.

Layer 5 Fitness, Probity & Insolvency

Disqualified directors, bankrupt individuals, and entities subject to insolvency proceedings sourced from national company registries and court records.

Layer 6 Ongoing Monitoring

All of the above, re-run daily for every enrolled entity. New hits trigger webhook alerts immediately. Compliance teams act on changes, not periodic manual re-checks.
