Consent Status Values

ActiveUser has given consent; processing is permitted.
WithdrawnUser exercised Right to Withdraw; processing must stop.
ExpiredConsent period has elapsed; re-consent required.
Pending RefreshConsent text updated; re-consent flow triggered.
DeclinedUser was shown the notice and actively refused.
Not CollectedUser exists but no consent record is on file.
