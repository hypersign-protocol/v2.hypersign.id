Use Cases

Who Uses Transaction Monitoring

Fintech & Neobanks

Monitor every payment from account opening through the customer lifecycle. Detect structuring, mule accounts, and behavioural anomalies before funds leave the platform.

Crypto Exchanges & VASPs

Screen every on-chain and off-chain transaction for sanctions exposure, darknet connections, and FATF Travel Rule compliance. Meet MiCA and AMLD6 from day one.

Payments & Remittance

Apply risk-based monitoring to cross-border transfers without adding friction for legitimate senders. Block high-risk corridors; auto-approve established payment patterns.

iGaming & Betting

Monitor deposits, withdrawals, and bonus abuse for problem gambling signals and AML patterns. Meet UK Gambling Commission, Malta Gaming Authority, and EU licensing requirements.

BNPL & Lending

Detect fraud and structuring at the application and repayment stages. Screen counterparty accounts before disbursement completes.

Banking & Credit Unions

Replace legacy batch monitoring with real-time decisioning. Reduce false positive alert volumes and eliminate manual data exports to external case management systems.
