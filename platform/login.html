Login & Authentication

Multiple ways to authenticate.
One seamless experience.

Give users the flexibility to sign in how they prefer: social login, email, PIN, or one-time passcode, while maintaining security and compliance across every session.

Supported Methods

Four ways in. One identity layer.

Google · GitHub · more

Social Login

Let users sign in with their existing Google or social accounts. Zero password management, instant trust, and high conversion on first visit.

  • One-tap authentication via OAuth 2.0
  • Supports Google, GitHub, and custom OAuth providers
  • Profile data pre-filled. No onboarding form needed.
  • Linked to Hypersign identity record automatically
Password · Magic Link

Email Login

Classic email-based login with secure password hashing or a passwordless magic link, whichever your users prefer.

  • Email + password with bcrypt hashing
  • Passwordless magic link sent to inbox
  • Optional 2FA via TOTP or SMS
  • Account recovery and email verification flow included
Numeric · Fast re-auth

PIN

Short numeric PIN for frictionless re-authentication, ideal for mobile apps, kiosk flows, and returning users who need quick access.

  • 4–8 digit configurable PIN length
  • Brute-force lockout after failed attempts
  • PIN tied to device. Not transferable.
  • Works alongside biometric step-up
One-time · SMS · Email

Passcode / OTP

One-time passcodes delivered via SMS or email. Passwordless and credential-free, great for users who avoid remembering passwords.

  • 6-digit OTP with configurable expiry
  • Delivered via SMS, email, or authenticator app
  • Time-based (TOTP) and counter-based (HOTP) support
  • No stored credentials. Zero breach surface.

Built-in Security

Every method. Every session. Secured.

Session Management
Configurable session TTL, refresh tokens, and forced logout across devices.
Multi-Factor Authentication
Layer a second factor on any login method: TOTP, SMS, or biometric.
Biometric Step-up
Trigger a liveness check at sensitive moments without a full re-login.
Audit Logs
Timestamped record of every login event, failure, and session action.
Rate Limiting & Lockout
Automatic throttling and account lockout after repeated failed attempts.
SSO Ready
SAML 2.0 and OIDC support for enterprise single sign-on integrations.
