Template matching, hologram detection, portrait integrity, and biographic tamper analysis then confirm it's genuine, not just readable — and capture quality is scored before any of this runs, so a bad photo never reaches extraction in the first place.

Screen captures, printed copies, and camera-roll images are blocked before any other check runs — only a physical document in natural lighting gets through. No forger can replicate a government signature; it's the highest assurance level available for document verification.

Fraud Prevention

200+ Signals to Protect Every ID Verification

Identity verification takes more than one check. Hypersign layers multiple checks so defeating one doesn't defeat the whole process.

Session-Level Signals

  • Duplicate face detection across all account sessions
  • Duplicate document number detection
  • Device & IP risk scoring (VPN, Tor, proxy, emulator)
  • Velocity checks (multiple submissions, same device)
  • Blocklist matching: face hash, document number, device fingerprint

Biometric Liveness & Face Match

  • Passive liveness: real person vs. printed photo or screen replay
  • Active liveness: challenge-response to defeat video replay
  • Deepfake detection: AI-generated faces and face-swap attacks
  • 1:1 face match: live selfie matched to document portrait
