The Challenge

Global onboarding isn't a KYC problem. It's an orchestration problem.

CBUAE (UAE), FCA DVS (UK), and MAS Notice 626 (SG) didn't create new tools. They exposed the gap between the tools that already exist.

Fragmented Vendor Stack

One vendor for documents. Another for biometrics. A third for AML. Each best-in-class individually — but together they create accountability gaps no single vendor owns.

No Unified Audit Trail

AMLD6 (EU), FCA MLR (UK), RBI KYC (IN), HKMA AML (HK) — all require coherent audit evidence. When it lives across four systems, producing it under regulatory time pressure is a risk in itself.

Rebuild Every Regulatory Cycle

DORA (EU), Consumer Duty (UK), AUSTRAC (AU), CBUAE (UAE) — each adds requirements. Every update forces changes across multiple vendor integrations rather than one workflow config.

Fintech Verticals

Every regulated fintech. One orchestration layer.

From neobanks to remittance providers, each product line carries specific obligations — all resolved through a single integration.

Neobanks & Digital Banks

Account opening in minutes demands fast, accurate KYC. Automated document and biometric checks replace manual review queues and meet central bank licensing requirements.

Payment Processors & E-Money

PSD2 and e-money regulations require verified customer identities before funds can move. Hypersign's automated KYC and AML checks satisfy these obligations without slowing payment flows.

BNPL & Consumer Lending

Buy-now-pay-later and personal loan providers must verify borrower identity and creditworthiness quickly. Real-time identity checks reduce default risk and meet consumer credit regulation.

P2P Lending Platforms

Both lenders and borrowers on peer-to-peer platforms require identity verification before funds are committed. Full KYC with AML screening protects every side of the transaction.

Wealth Management & Robo-Advisors

MiFID II and local securities regulations mandate investor identity verification and suitability assessment. Hypersign verifies investors at onboarding with a full audit trail.

Insurance Technology

Insurtech platforms need to confirm policyholder identity to prevent fraudulent claims. Biometric verification and document checks at policy issuance create a verifiable record from day one.

Crypto & Digital Asset Platforms

Exchanges, wallets, and stablecoin providers face the strictest KYC and AML mandates. Hypersign covers Travel Rule requirements, wallet screening, and cross-chain identity portability.

Remittance & FX Platforms

Cross-border money transfer platforms must verify senders and recipients against FATF guidelines. Hypersign's global document coverage spans 189+ countries for end-to-end compliance.
