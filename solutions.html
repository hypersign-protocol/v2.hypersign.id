Solution 03

Age Verification

Confirm a user meets your age threshold without storing unnecessary PII. Hypersign reads date of birth from verified documents, runs liveness, and returns a simple pass/fail with an audit trail.

  • Configurable minimum age (18+, 21+, or custom)
  • Selective disclosure share “over 18” without full date of birth
  • Document + biometric checks in one hosted flow
  • Timestamped results for gaming, alcohol, and regulated content

Aadhaar Verification
