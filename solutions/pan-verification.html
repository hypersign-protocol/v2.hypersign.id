Regulatory Frameworks

Income Tax Act, 1961NSDL / Income Tax Dept. RecordsIndia DPDP Act, 2023

Stated plainly: PAN Verification checks records sourced from NSDL / Income Tax Department data, issued under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Unlike Aadhaar eKYC, PAN verification is a stateless record lookup, no AUA/KUA-style licence is required on your side to consume it. The service runs on infrastructure hosted entirely in India (GCP), and verification data is not retained beyond what's needed to complete the check and generate the audit record.
