How it works

Verify the human, not the wallet

Wallet-based voting can be gamed by anyone willing to split funds across addresses. Hypersign verifies the person once and issues a credential that proves eligibility without exposing who they are or how they voted.

1Participant completes identity verification and liveness check once
2Hypersign issues a Verifiable Credential proving unique-human status
3The credential is presented to the governance contract to unlock voting eligibility
4Selective disclosure ensures only the eligibility fact is shared, not the underlying identity
