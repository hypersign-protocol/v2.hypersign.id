How it works

Compliance sequenced the way regulators check it

UKGC and MGA both audit the sequence, not just the outcome: was identity verified before gambling was permitted, was the player screened before a self-exclusion breach occurred, was source-of-funds requested before — not after — a suspicious pattern. Hypersign runs the checks in that order automatically.

1Player completes identity and age verification at registration, before any gambling is permitted
2Player is screened against applicable self-exclusion registries and sanctions/PEP lists
3A risk rating is assigned and enhanced due diligence triggers automatically for high-risk profiles
4Deposits and withdrawals above your defined thresholds trigger source-of-funds review
5Player status is re-checked on an ongoing basis as registries, risk ratings, and regulations update
