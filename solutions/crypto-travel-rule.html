How it works

FATF, MiCA, and FinCEN Travel Rule requirements — one data-capture flow

Travel rule data doesn't need a separate collection step. Hypersign captures the required originator and beneficiary fields as part of standard identity verification, then attaches them automatically once a transfer crosses your reporting threshold — whether the applicable requirement is FATF Recommendation 16 globally, MiCA in the EU, or FinCEN's Bank Secrecy Act Travel Rule in the US.

1Originator identity is verified and travel-rule fields are captured at onboarding
2Transfer is evaluated against your jurisdiction's reporting threshold
3Above threshold, originator/beneficiary data is packaged for VASP-to-VASP exchange
4Sanctions screening runs on both parties before the transfer is cleared
