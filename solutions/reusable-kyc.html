How it works

One verification. Every partner.

01

Verify Once

The user completes KYC or KYB on your platform. Hypersign runs document capture, liveness detection, and AML screening then issues a signed W3C Verifiable Credential as proof of their verified status.

→
02

User Consents

When the user joins a partner platform, they see a single plain-language consent screen. One tap shares their credential. No re-upload, no new selfie, no waiting for manual review.

→
03

Partner Accepts

The partner receives a verified result identity data, document images, and verification outcomes or re-runs verification against their own risk policy using the shared credential as the source.

Reusable Credentials

One credential. Accepted everywhere.

Hypersign issues a W3C Verifiable Credential the moment a user passes KYC or KYB. That credential travels with the user cryptographically signed, tamper-proof, and instantly verifiable by any trusted partner in your network without calling back to you.

  • W3C VC standard interoperable with any compliant verifier
  • Credential includes identity data, document type, and verification outcomes
  • Partners accept directly or re-run against their own risk policy
  • Instant credential revocation if fraud is detected

User Consent

Explicit consent at every share.

Users always approve sharing before anything moves. The consent screen uses plain language no buried checkboxes. Every consent event is timestamped and added to the immutable audit trail your compliance team can pull for any review.

  • Explicit opt-in before any data is shared with a partner
  • Selective disclosure share only the fields the partner needs
  • Consent withdrawal supported; partners notified instantly
  • Immutable consent log for GDPR, DPDP, and audit readiness

AML Compliance

AML reliance provision, built in.

Sharing verified KYC results between institutions is recognized under FinCEN's CIP reliance provision and equivalent global frameworks. Hypersign structures every share to satisfy these requirements no legal tri-party agreements needed to get started.

  • Structured for FinCEN CIP reliance and global AML equivalents
  • AML and sanctions screening included in every issued credential
  • Partners receive the same audit-grade evidence as a full verification
  • Ongoing screening flag users who appear on watchlists post-verification

Privacy by Design

Zero raw PII in transit.

Partners verify the cryptographic proof, not the underlying data. Raw identity documents and biometric records never leave the Hypersign identity vault. You define the exact scope of each share from a simple pass/fail signal to a full verified record.

  • Selective disclosure share only what the partner actually requires
  • Raw documents and biometrics stay in encrypted identity vault
  • Zero-knowledge proofs available for privacy-critical use cases
  • Data residency control region-locked storage on request
