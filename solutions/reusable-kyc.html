How it works

One KYC verification. Every partner.

01

Verify Once

The user completes KYC verification or KYB verification on your platform. Hypersign runs document capture, liveness detection, and AML screening then issues a signed W3C Verifiable Credential as proof of their verified status.

→
02

User Consents

When the user joins a partner platform, they see a single plain-language consent screen. One tap shares their credential. No re-upload, no new selfie, no waiting for manual review.

→
03

Partner Accepts

The partner receives a verified result identity data, document images, and verification outcomes or re-runs verification against their own risk policy using the shared credential as the source.

Reusable Credentials

One credential. Accepted everywhere.

Hypersign issues a W3C Verifiable Credential the moment a user passes customer ID verification for KYC or KYB. That credential travels with the user cryptographically signed, tamper-proof, and instantly verifiable by any trusted partner in your network without calling back to you.

  • W3C VC standard interoperable with any compliant verifier
  • Credential includes identity data, document type, and verification outcomes
  • Partners accept directly or re-run against their own risk policy
  • Instant credential revocation if fraud is detected

User Consent

Explicit consent at every share.

Users always approve sharing before anything moves. The consent screen uses plain language no buried checkboxes. Every consent event is timestamped and added to the immutable audit trail your compliance team can pull for any review.

  • Explicit opt-in before any data is shared with a partner
  • Selective disclosure share only the fields the partner needs
  • Consent withdrawal supported; partners notified instantly
  • Immutable consent log for GDPR, DPDP, and audit readiness

AML Compliance

AML compliance and CIP reliance, built in.

Sharing verified KYC results between institutions satisfies FinCEN's CIP reliance provision and equivalent global AML frameworks. Every credential carries built-in sanctions screening and ongoing compliance monitoring no tri-party agreements required.

  • FinCEN CIP reliance provision, plus global AML equivalents
  • Sanctions and PEP screening included in every issued credential
  • Audit-grade evidence, equivalent to a full verification
  • Ongoing compliance monitoring flags watchlist matches automatically

Privacy by Design

PII protection, built into every identity verification.

Partners verify a cryptographic proof, not raw PII. Data minimization is built into the architecture: raw documents and biometrics stay in the encrypted Hypersign identity vault, and you control exactly what each partner sees.

  • Data minimization by design only the fields a partner needs
  • Encrypted identity vault raw documents and biometrics never leave it
  • Zero-knowledge proof identity checks for privacy-critical flows
  • Data residency region-locked storage on request
