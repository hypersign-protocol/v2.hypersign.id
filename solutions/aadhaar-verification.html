OTP-Based eKYC · Via Sub-AUA Partnership

Live UIDAI data, delivered over OTP.

An OTP is sent to the mobile number linked with the Aadhaar record. Once verified, Hypersign retrieves the demographic data live from UIDAI through our licensed Sub-AUA partner, so you get authoritative data without holding your own AUA/KUA licence.

Response Structure

A Typed Result Your Backend Can Act On

Every verification, OTP or offline, returns a consistent JSON payload and a webhook event. No ambiguous status strings.

Verification Status Values

VerifiedOTP or offline verification passed; face match succeeded.
OTP ExpiredThe OTP was not entered within the validity window. User can request a new one.
Invalid SignatureThe QR/XML digital signature could not be verified against UIDAI's public key.
Face Match FailedThe live selfie did not match the Aadhaar photo within the configured threshold.
Consent RequiredVerification cannot proceed until explicit consent is captured.
PendingUser has not yet completed the OTP or QR scan step.
