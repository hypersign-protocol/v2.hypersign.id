How it works

Fiat on-ramp and off-ramp, screened the same way

Buyer identity and destination wallet are screened together on the way in, so a clean KYC result can't be paired with a high-risk wallet without triggering review — and the same pipeline runs in reverse for off-ramp (crypto-to-fiat) sellers and their payout destination, since the compliance obligation doesn't disappear just because the direction of the transaction flips.

1Buyer or seller completes KYC: document, liveness, and face match
2Destination wallet (on-ramp) or payout account (off-ramp) is screened for sanctions and risk exposure
3Combined risk score is returned before the transaction settles
4Flagged sessions route to manual review or step-up verification
