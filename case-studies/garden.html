Identity Verification for a DeFi Protocol Wallet Ownership Proved, Fraudulent Recovery Claims Blocked

The Challenge

After a solver exploit, there was no identity layer, no audit trail, and no onboarding process. Recovery claimants could not prove wallet ownership.

The Result

Identity verification combined with wallet-signature binding created cryptographic proof of ownership enabling legitimate recovery claims while blocking fraudulent ones.

DeFi Protocol came to Hypersign facing a real compliance challenge: a solver exploit left funds inaccessible and ownership disputed. Without any identity or wallet-binding layer, users claiming recovery couldn't prove they were the legitimate wallet owners and bad actors were attempting to claim funds that weren't theirs.

The Approach

Hypersign implemented a two-layer verification flow: first, standard identity verification to confirm who the user was; second, wallet-signature binding to cryptographically tie that verified identity to a specific on-chain address. Only users who could pass both checks matching their verified identity to a signed message from the claiming wallet were eligible for recovery. The entire process created a tamper-evident audit trail.

Key Outcomes

  • ✓Cryptographic proof of ownership established for every legitimate claimant
  • ✓Fraudulent recovery attempts blocked at the verification layer
  • ✓Audit trail created for regulatory and legal review
  • ✓Legitimate fund recovery completed without manual case-by-case review

What's Next

The protocol is building identity-based access controls into their core flow requiring wallet-signature binding for all high-value transactions, not just recovery scenarios.
