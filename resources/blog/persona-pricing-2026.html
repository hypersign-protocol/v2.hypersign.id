Persona Pricing in 2026: What It Actually Costs

Persona publishes a real number for one tier, Essential at $250/month, then stops. Here's what Persona actually charges per verification, what the separate Startup Program includes, what sits behind the unpriced Growth and Enterprise tiers, and how the total compares to Hypersign's flat rate at volume.

Vikram Anand Bhushan, Co-founder & CPO, Hypersign·September 1, 2026·7 min read

"Persona pricing" turns up one real number and a lot of "let's talk." Persona publishes its Essential tier rate openly, but Growth and Enterprise, the plans most teams actually land on once they need deeper case management or AML volume, sit behind a sales conversation with no published figure.

This post lays out exactly what Persona's own pricing page and help center publish, what the separate Startup Program actually includes, and what a team ends up paying at a representative volume, cross-checked against Persona's help center, third-party pricing trackers (Capterra, Vendr), and the figures already published on Hypersign's own Persona comparison page rather than a single secondhand summary.

The Free Startup Program Is a Different Plan, Not a Free Tier of Essential

It's easy to conflate the two, but Persona runs them separately. Essential is a paid plan with a free allowance built in; the Startup Program is a distinct, application-only track.

  • Who qualifies. Eligible SMBs, venture-backed startups, and independent developers can apply; Persona states it welcomes applications worldwide.
  • What's included. Up to 12 months of free access, with 500 free Government ID (plus optional selfie) verifications and 1,000 Relay services per month, then $1 per additional service, cheaper than Essential's $1.50 overage rate.
  • What happens after 12 months. The account is automatically deactivated unless it's transitioned to a paid plan. This isn't a permanent free tier, it's a 12-month runway.
  • Product scope. The Startup Program covers Persona's "most trusted products." Add-on templates like the KYC + Crypto Watchlist screen used for Web3 onboarding aren't included at this tier; they show up starting at Essential. Covered in more depth in Persona alternatives for Web3 and DeFi onboarding.

A separate 60-day free trial is also referenced by third-party pricing trackers (Vendr, PricingSaaS) as available ahead of any paid or Startup Program commitment. That figure isn't confirmed directly on Persona's own pricing page as of this post's publication date, treat it as reported rather than verified before relying on it for planning.

Reading the Fine Print on Persona Cost

Essential's $250/month rate is tied to a 12-month contract, not a month-to-month plan. That's a meaningful difference from a pay-as-you-go model: committing to Essential locks in the rate but also the term, and dropping below 500 verifications a month for a stretch doesn't lower the bill.

The $1.50 overage rate on Essential compares to $1 per additional service on the Startup Program, a detail easy to miss if you're pricing off the standard plan page instead of the startup-specific terms.

Which Pricing Model Actually Fits

Persona's Essential tier is a reasonable starting point below roughly 500 to 650 verifications a month, especially for a US-centric fintech or marketplace team that values its no-code Workflow Builder and is comfortable with a 12-month contract. Its strengths outside pricing are real: a well-regarded no-code builder, strong document coverage, and (per its own reporting) a $2B valuation off a $200M Series D that signals long-term platform investment.

  • Steady volume under ~650 verifications/month and want a no-code builder? Persona's Essential tier is priced for exactly that range.
  • Volume above that, or need KYB and consent management self-serve without a Growth/Enterprise sales cycle? That's the specific gap Hypersign's flat-rate model is built to close.
  • An early-stage team that qualifies for Persona's Startup Program? The 12-month free runway is worth applying for regardless of which platform you land on longer-term, just plan for the deactivation date.

FAQ

How much does Persona cost per verification?

Persona's Essential plan runs $250/month on a 12-month contract, with 500 free services included and $1.50 per additional service past that. Growth and Enterprise, which add Graph link-analysis, Cases, and KYB, are custom-quoted with no published rate.

Is Persona free to use?

Not permanently for most teams. Persona's separate Startup Program gives eligible startups up to 12 months free (500 free Government ID verifications plus 1,000 Relay services a month, then $1 per additional service), but the account is automatically deactivated after 12 months unless it moves to a paid plan.

What isn't included in Persona's published Essential pricing?

Business verification (KYB), Graph fraud-ring detection, Cases investigation workflows, and higher-volume AML and ongoing monitoring all sit behind the unpriced Growth or Enterprise tiers. None appear as a self-serve line item on Essential.

How does Persona vs Hypersign pricing compare?

At 1,200 verifications a month, Persona's Essential tier runs roughly $1,300 against Hypersign's flat $900 at the same volume. Below about 667 verifications a month, Persona's flat $250 base is actually cheaper; above it, Hypersign's flat rate pulls ahead.
