7 Biometric Verification & Liveness Detection Providers, Compared (2026)

We compared seven biometric verification and liveness detection providers on liveness type, PAD/iBeta certification, and real per-check pricing, including the four that won't publish a rate card.

Hypersign Team·August 25, 2026·9 min read

Every KYC vendor's homepage claims biometric verification and liveness detection. Almost none of them say what type of liveness it actually is, a single passive selfie or a full active challenge-response check, what certification backs the anti-spoofing claim, or what a check costs once the free trial runs out. We put together this list to fix that: seven biometric verification and liveness detection providers, including Hypersign, where our team works, compared on liveness type, PAD/iBeta certification, and real per-check pricing.

Biometric Verification Providers: Bundled Platforms vs. Point Specialists

Biometric verification providers split into two real categories, and mixing them up is where most shopping lists go wrong. Full-stack KYC platforms like Veriff, Sumsub, and Jumio bundle biometric verification, face match plus liveness, into a broader document-onboarding product. Pure-play specialists like FaceTec, iProov, and Regula sell the biometric layer alone, as an SDK or API you wire into a stack you already have.

Neither is objectively better. A team that needs a full onboarding flow, document capture included, usually wants the bundled kind. A team that already has document verification running and needs a stronger or more certified liveness layer specifically is shopping for a specialist instead.

This list stays scoped to that biometric identity verification system layer on purpose: liveness type, certification, deepfake resistance, and biometric-specific pricing. If you need the fuller document-coverage comparison, our guide to identity verification providers covers OCR accuracy, country coverage, and NFC passport reading instead. Buyers shopping for biometric verification specifically usually care about three things:

  • Liveness type. Active liveness asks the user to do something, blink, turn their head, and confirms presence by verifying that action happened. Passive liveness runs silently on a single capture. Most vendors support one as the default and offer the other as an option.
  • Certification. ISO/IEC 30107-3 Presentation Attack Detection (PAD) testing, ideally Level 2 from an accredited lab like iBeta, is the difference between a marketing claim and an independently verified one.
  • Pricing model. Some vendors bundle biometric checks into a published per-verification rate. Others sell quote-only, which tells you a lot about how long you'll wait to find out if a vendor even fits your budget.

7 Biometric Verification & Liveness Detection Providers, Compared

These seven cover both categories: three bundled KYC platforms, three pure-play biometric specialists, and Hypersign. We list Hypersign last, not because it belongs last, but because the fairest way to compare a vendor you work for is to make its own team explain it after everyone else has had a turn.

1. Veriff

Veriff bundles biometric verification and liveness into every self-serve tier, without a sales call to get started. It's one of the few names on this list with fully published pricing at the entry level.

Key Features

  • Biometric and liveness checks bundled into every tier, including the cheapest one
  • Self-serve signup with no sales call required
  • Video recording and specialist review available on higher tiers
  • Extended data-retention and ongoing-monitoring add-ons

Pricing

The Essential tier runs $0.80 per verification with a $49 a month minimum, biometric and liveness checks included, 3-month data retention. Plus runs $1.39 per verification ($99 a month minimum, adds specialist review and video recording), and Premium runs $1.89 per verification ($209 a month minimum). A 15-day free trial includes 50 live verification sessions, no credit card required. Confirm current rates on Veriff's own pricing page before budgeting against them.

Real Use Cases

A marketplace that wants biometric verification live this week, not after a procurement cycle, can start on the Essential tier the same day. A team that later needs specialist human review on flagged sessions can move up to Plus without switching vendors.

2. Sumsub

Sumsub bundles Advanced Liveness and face matching into its base plan, and it's a common choice for teams that need biometric verification and AML screening from a single vendor.

Key Features

  • Advanced Liveness detection with 3D face mapping
  • Face matching between selfie and document photo
  • Both active and passive facial biometric liveness supported
  • Published tiered pricing, unusual in this category

Pricing

The Basic plan runs $1.35 per verification with a $149 a month minimum, and includes ID verification plus liveness and face match. The Compliance plan runs $1.85 per verification ($299 a month minimum, adds AML screening). A 14-day free trial includes 50 free checks. Confirm current tiers directly with Sumsub, since which features sit behind which plan shifts over time.

Real Use Cases

A crypto exchange that needs biometric verification plus AML screening in one vendor can start on Basic and move to Compliance once ongoing monitoring becomes a real requirement rather than a nice-to-have.

3. Jumio

Jumio sells Premium Liveness Detection as a distinct product tier, built around a patented active-illumination technique rather than a generic liveness check bolted onto document verification.

Key Features

  • Patented active-illumination liveness technique
  • In-house liveness engine, not a third-party or white-labeled one
  • ISO/IEC 30107-3 Level 2 PAD conformance, independently tested by the NIST/NVLAP-accredited iBeta lab, per Jumio's own published testing announcement
  • Purpose-built defenses against deepfakes and video injection attacks

Pricing

Jumio doesn't publish a rate card for Premium Liveness Detection or its broader platform. You'll need a sales quote to get an actual number, which is typical for large enterprise-focused vendors in this category.

Real Use Cases

A large, regulated enterprise that already runs Jumio through an existing vendor security review, or one that specifically needs certified resistance to injection and deepfake attacks, is the clearest fit. The sales cycle rarely suits an early-stage team in a hurry.

4. FaceTec

FaceTec is a pure-play biometric specialist. 3D Face Liveness, marketed as ZoOm, is the entire product, not one feature bundled into a wider KYC platform, and it comes with one of the more extensively documented certification trails in the category.

Key Features

  • 3D Face Liveness certified at both ISO 30107-3 Level 1 and Level 2, per FaceTec's own published third-party testing report
  • iBeta PAD and IAD lab testing across levels 1 through 5
  • SDK-first, built to drop into a stack you already have rather than replace it
  • Unlimited photo ID scans for KYC/AML included free for customers and partners

Pricing

FaceTec is quote-only. Billing runs per 3D Liveness check or per user, with a minimum monthly commitment, and no published tiers. A free demo and sample apps are available for internal proof-of-concept and developer testing before any commercial conversation.

Real Use Cases

A team that already has document verification running and wants to add or swap in a heavily certified liveness layer, without adopting a whole new onboarding platform, is FaceTec's core use case.

5. iProov

iProov calls its approach Genuine Presence Assurance, and it's built for enterprise and government buyers where certification depth matters as much as the check itself.

Key Features

  • iBeta and ISO/IEC 30107-3 PAD testing
  • NIST SP 800-63-4 alignment and eIDAS Level of Assurance High
  • FIDO Face Verification Certified
  • SOC 2 Type II certified

Pricing

iProov doesn't publish a pricing page. Every engagement runs through a sales conversation, which tracks with its enterprise and government-heavy customer base.

Real Use Cases

A government agency or bank that needs the specific certifications regulators ask for by name, eIDAS High assurance or FIDO certification among them, has fewer vendors to choose from than this list otherwise suggests, and iProov is built for exactly that shortlist.

6. Regula (Face SDK)

Regula's Face SDK is a biometric specialist built SDK-first, with an on-device and on-premise deployment option that matters for buyers who can't run a SaaS-only biometric check at all.

Key Features

  • 1:1 face matching between a live selfie and a document photo
  • Active and passive liveness detection in the same SDK
  • On-device, on-premise, and server-side deployment options
  • Integrates directly with Regula's Document Reader SDK for a combined document-plus-biometric flow

Pricing

No pricing is published on Regula's product page. Getting a number means requesting a demo and talking to sales.

Real Use Cases

A buyer in a jurisdiction that legally requires biometric data to stay in-country, common across GCC and parts of Southeast Asia, rules out SaaS-only vendors outright. Regula's on-premise option is built for that constraint specifically, not as a workaround.

7. Hypersign

Hypersign, where our team works, bundles biometric verification into its base KYC platform rather than selling it as a separate specialist SDK. Face match, active liveness, and passive liveness are all included in the standard bundle, not add-ons priced separately, which matters if you're comparing this list's bundled platforms against its specialists and trying to work out which model actually fits.

Key Features

  • 1:1 face match, active liveness, and passive liveness all included in the base bundle, not sold as add-ons
  • Deepfake detection and multi-layer anti-spoofing built into the same verification pass
  • Biometric Authentication available as a step-up add-on for re-verifying returning users
  • Audit-ready for iBeta Level 1 PAD certification, currently in progress and not yet completed, alongside SOC 2 Type I and ISO/IEC 27001:2022
  • Bundled with document verification across 189-plus countries in the same integration, if you need both

Pricing

Pricing is published, not sales-gated. The Starter plan runs $500 per quarter for the first year, with 100 checks included per quarter, then drops to $0.75 per verification pay-as-you-go with no minimum from year two, biometric checks included in that rate rather than metered separately. There's a free sandbox to test the integration before any commercial conversation starts.

Real Use Cases

A fintech that wants biometric verification and document verification in one integration, instead of stitching a specialist liveness SDK onto a separate document-check vendor, can run both through Hypersign's single API and one contract.

A team that outgrew a specialist SDK's narrow scope, and needs the biometric layer to sit inside a broader KYC decision instead of returning an isolated pass or fail, is the clearer fit for the bundled model over a point solution.

Pricing Overview

  • Sumsub: $149 to $299 a month minimum, plus $1.35 to $1.85 per verification, biometric checks bundled
  • Jumio: not published, quote-only for Premium Liveness Detection
  • FaceTec: not published, quote-only, per-check or per-user with a monthly minimum, free demo/POC

Which Biometric Verification Provider Should You Actually Pick

If you need the specific certifications a government contract or bank compliance review asks for by name, iProov's certification list is hard to match. If biometric data has to stay in-country by law, Regula's on-premise option covers a constraint the SaaS-only vendors on this list simply can't. If you already have document verification running and want the most independently certified liveness layer as a drop-in SDK, FaceTec is built for exactly that.
