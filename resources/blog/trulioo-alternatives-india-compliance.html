Best Trulioo Alternatives for India-Compliant KYC (2026)

Trulioo's own site claims 195 countries and 450+ data sources, but nothing on it, including its India KYC blog post, mentions CKYC or the DPDP Act. Here's how seven real Trulioo alternatives compare, including the one built for DPDP-regulated Indian fintechs.

Hypersign Compliance Team·August 27, 2026·7 min read

Trulioo's homepage pitch is "All Your Identity Verification Combined": 195 countries, 450+ global and local data sources, 14,000+ document types, and 700M+ verifiable business entities, checked against 6,000+ watchlists and 20,000+ adverse media sources. That's real breadth, and it's why Trulioo shows up on almost every global KYC shortlist. It's also why compliance teams building specifically for India start searching for Trulioo alternatives: nothing on Trulioo's own site, including its dedicated India KYC blog post, mentions CKYC, the DPDP Act, or any India-specific regulatory rail.

That gap is the reason this list exists. Below are seven real Trulioo competitors, priced and sourced from each vendor's own site or Hypersign's own published research, including the one built specifically for DPDP-regulated Indian fintechs.

What to Look for in a Trulioo Alternative

Before comparing vendors, it helps to know what actually separates a good fit from a bad one:

  • Breadth versus India-specific depth. A network of 450+ global data sources doesn't automatically include CKYC access or an Aadhaar-linked verification rail built for Indian regulatory requirements.
  • DPDP compliant KYC, not just GDPR language. Purpose-bound consent, an audit trail, and a right-to-erasure path are DPDP requirements, and most identity verification vendors don't ship any of that as a product feature.
  • Published pricing versus a sales quote. A rate card on a vendor's site is a decent proxy for how easy it is to start with as a smaller team.
  • AML and KYB placement. Some vendors bundle sanctions screening and business verification into the base rate; others gate both behind a custom Enterprise quote.
  • Reuse that isn't network-locked. A verified identity should be reusable across compliant partners, not just other customers of the same vendor.

Where Trulioo Actually Fits

Trulioo's numbers are real and sourced directly from its own site: 195 countries, 450+ global and local data sources, 14,000+ document types, 700M+ verifiable business entities, and watchlist screening across 6,000+ lists plus 20,000+ adverse media sources, completed in under 5.2 seconds on average. It holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001:2022 certification, and its GlobalGateway model layers multiple data sources into a single verification call rather than relying on one proprietary engine.

None of that makes Trulioo a bad product. But its own India KYC blog post only lists Aadhaar and PAN as accepted document types, nothing about CKYC integration, DPDP consent handling, or any India-specific compliance rail, and the same gap holds on its regulatory compliance page, which names FinCEN, FCA, FINTRAC, CySEC, and MAS but not a single Indian regulator. Pricing isn't published either: GlobalGateway runs on a pay-as-you-go, per-verification model priced by country and data source, and third-party estimates put per-check costs between $1 and $3, with entry-level engagements reportedly starting near $99 a month before usage.

1. Hypersign

Hypersign is built as an India KYC provider first, not a 195-country network with India folded in as one more market. Document OCR across 189+ countries and 14,000+ document types, biometric liveness and face match, a decision engine, and consent management are all included on the Starter plan, no Enterprise quote required for any of it.

KYB is a self-serve add-on at $20 per business, and AML screening across 1,300+ watchlists is a self-serve add-on too, both without a sales call. Every verified identity is issued as a W3C DID and Verifiable Credential, so it's reusable with any compliant partner instead of locked inside one vendor's data network, and selective disclosure lets a check reveal just an over-18 flag instead of a full birthdate.

The part that maps directly to what Trulioo's own site doesn't mention: native purpose-bound consent capture, an immutable audit trail, and a right-to-erasure path ship as part of the product. Pricing is $500 per quarter for the first year (about 100 checks per quarter included), then $0.75 per verification flat with no minimum from year two. For a compliance team typing trulioo vs hypersign into a search bar, that's the direct trade: 195 countries of undifferentiated breadth against a product built around India's specific regulatory rail.

2. Sumsub

Sumsub covers 220+ countries and 14,000+ document types, screens against 50,000+ AML data sources, and connects to 2,100+ VASPs for Travel Rule, breadth that rivals Trulioo's own numbers. Its Basic plan runs $1.35 per verification with a $149 monthly minimum, and the Compliance plan, which adds AML monitoring, runs $1.85 per verification with a $299 monthly minimum, with KYB and deeper compliance workflows gated behind a custom Enterprise quote.

Like Trulioo, nothing on Sumsub's site makes a DPDP-specific claim, so a team onboarding Indian users is on its own for consent and erasure handling either way.

3. Entrust (formerly Onfido)

Entrust completed its acquisition of Onfido, one of the longest-running identity verification vendors, in April 2024, folding it into the wider Entrust Identity Security portfolio. Pre-acquisition, Onfido reported $140M+ in annual revenue and 1,200+ customers, so the document and liveness coverage behind Entrust's offering has real scale, backed further by Entrust's own PKI and certificate business.

Pricing isn't published; every deal routes through a sales quote, and there's no DPDP-specific claim on Entrust's site either. It's a reasonable Trulioo competitor if you're already buying other Entrust security infrastructure and want identity verification on the same contract.

4. Jumio

Jumio positions itself around continuous identity intelligence rather than a one-time check, built on its Identity Graph, a network it says spans 30M+ identities used to catch patterns across legitimate users and fraud rings. It supports 5,000+ ID types and includes selfie.DONE, a reusable verification path for returning users.

Pricing is quote-based only, with no rate card published anywhere on Jumio's site, and no DPDP-specific compliance claim either. Jumio's cross-transaction risk graph is a real strength for fraud-heavy verticals, it's just not a self-serve or India-compliance-first product, the same trade-off Trulioo makes.

5. GBG

GBG has been in the identity data business longer than most vendors on this list and leans on its own global data-source network for address and identity checks, the same breadth-first model Trulioo runs on. It shows up consistently in analyst commentary and identity-verification roundups.

Pricing is entirely usage-based and entirely private, no rate card exists publicly, and premium data-source access can add further cost on top of the base quote. Like Trulioo, GBG's strength is its network, not a published India-specific compliance rail.

6. HyperVerge

HyperVerge is the most India-focused name on this list, built around passive liveness ("no head turns, no OTPs, just a selfie") and DeepfakeSafe detection certified to ISO 30107-3 Level 2. It claims 500+ enterprise customers and 2B+ verifications, and markets "100% RBI-compliant onboarding" specifically for NBFCs and banks.

That RBI claim is worth reading carefully: it's a banking-license onboarding claim, not a DPDP data-protection certification, and no DPDP-specific compliance language appears on HyperVerge's site despite the India focus. Pricing isn't published, every plan routes to a demo request.

7. AU10TIX

AU10TIX is one of the oldest players in document forensics, with a fraud-pattern dataset built up over nearly two decades that shows in its forgery-detection accuracy, a strong choice for high-volume, high-fraud-risk onboarding at enterprise scale. Its tiers (Basic KYC, Enhanced KYC, Enterprise) carry a stated $500 monthly minimum, but there's no published per-verification rate beyond that.

AU10TIX's document-forensics depth is real, but like Trulioo, its coverage story is global rather than India-specific, so a DPDP consent and erasure layer would still need to be built or bought separately.

Choosing the Right Trulioo Alternative

If your user base is genuinely global and unpredictable across 195 countries, Trulioo's own coverage model, or GBG's similar data-network approach, is built for exactly that. If deep AML and Travel Rule tooling for a high-volume exchange is the priority, Sumsub's maturity is hard to beat. If you're already inside the Entrust ecosystem, staying there has integration advantages, and if continuous fraud-graph intelligence matters more than India-specific compliance, Jumio and AU10TIX are worth a serious look.

But if your compliance scope includes DPDP-regulated Indian users specifically, and you want consent capture, an audit trail, and reusable credentials included rather than bolted on through a second vendor, that's the specific gap none of the other six trulioo competitors here address, HyperVerge's India focus included, since even its RBI claim stops short of a DPDP-specific commitment.
