FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

An identity verification platform is a unified infrastructure layer that handles the full lifecycle of KYC and AML compliance — document checks, biometric liveness, business verification, sanctions screening, consent management, and audit trails — in a single integration. Hypersign provides all of these natively, so compliance teams don't need to stitch together separate vendors.
Most KYC vendors verify identity once and store raw data centrally. Hypersign goes further: it issues reusable verifiable credentials, captures explicit consent at every verification, stores identity in encrypted user-controlled vaults, and aligns with eIDAS 2.0 and the EUDI Wallet. You get full compliance infrastructure — not a point solution.
Hypersign supports 14,000+ document types across 189+ countries, including passports, national ID cards, driver licences, and residence permits. OCR, MRZ parsing, barcode reading, and NFC chip authentication are supported on applicable document types.
Yes. Hypersign builds consent capture, data minimisation, and purpose-linked data use into every verification flow. Timestamped audit logs prove compliance at any point in time. Lifecycle management in the identity vault supports GDPR's right to erasure and India's DPDP Act requirements.
Reusable KYC means a user verifies once and Hypersign issues a cryptographically signed verifiable credential. That credential is reused across any Hypersign-connected platform — so users never repeat the same check. This reduces onboarding drop-off, cuts per-user costs over time, and directly supports the eIDAS 2.0 EUDI Wallet standard.
Ongoing KYC is continuous monitoring of a user's verification status after the initial check. When an identity document on file expires, the user's status is automatically flagged, a webhook notification is sent, and a re-verification flow is triggered — keeping records compliant with AMLD6, eIDAS 2.0, and national AML frameworks without manual intervention.
Hypersign's KYB platform orchestrates full business onboarding: company document collection, director verification, UBO mapping, shareholder KYC, and ongoing AML monitoring — all in structured workflows with a complete audit trail. Registry lookups cover 200+ jurisdictions.
Teams can go live in minutes using Hypersign's no-code hosted verifier page. For custom integration, modular REST APIs and SDKs for KYC, KYB, AML, consent, and credentials are available. Full API integration typically takes days, not months.
Hypersign is audit-ready for SOC 2 Type I, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and iBeta Level 1 PAD, with those certifications currently in progress, and is aligned with GDPR, DORA, MiCA, AMLD6, eIDAS 2.0, and the FATF Travel Rule. Spain's Tesoro, SEPBLAC, and Banco de España have formally recognised Hypersign's verification process as safer than in-person verification.
