What HyperVerge Actually Is

An emerging-market eKYC specialist with patented liveness tech.

HyperVerge built its reputation on document OCR, biometric liveness (patented, ISO 30107-3 Level 2 certified), and agent-assisted or agentless video KYC, tuned for India, Southeast Asia, and Africa's onboarding conditions. It claims high accuracy at scale and has won industry recognition for its face-recognition technology.

Independent comparisons describe its scope as centered on onboarding verification, OCR, face match, video KYC, rather than full lifecycle fraud and compliance orchestration. We found no consent-management, identity-vault, or reusable/portable-credential product in its public materials, and pricing is enterprise/sales-led with no public rate card.

Choosing Between Them

Neither Is Wrong. They Fit Different Teams.

Choose HyperVerge if

  • Your primary markets are India, Southeast Asia, or Africa, and you want a proven, cost-effective eKYC and video-KYC point solution.
  • Patented, independently-certified biometric liveness accuracy is your top evaluation criterion.
  • You're comfortable handling AML depth, consent, and identity reuse as separate initiatives outside the platform.
  • You're fine with enterprise/sales-led pricing and onboarding.

Choose Hypersign if

  • You need onboarding verification plus AML, consent, and reusable-credential infrastructure in one integration.
  • Your compliance footprint spans emerging markets and the EU or other eIDAS/GDPR-relevant regions.
  • You want transparent, published self-serve pricing instead of a quote-only sales process.
  • You want portable credentials on open W3C DID/VC standards as part of the same verification flow.
